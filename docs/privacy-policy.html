Privacy Policy for Ihsan

Last Updated: September 2026
Summary: Ihsan is designed with privacy-by-design. All your daily habits, spiritual reflections, and check-ins are stored 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Ihsan operates as a private, offline-first personal reflection and habit tracking application:

  • We do not require user registration, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not collect personal tracking information or location data.
  • We do not track your usage with third-party analytics or behavioral cookies.
  • We do not display advertisements.

2. Local Data Storage & Persistence

All data generated within the app—including completed daily deeds, heart state check-ins, written evening reflections, and weekly/monthly intentions (Niyyah)—is stored exclusively on your device's local SQLite database.

3. Data Deletion & Control

You have full ownership of your data. You may at any time clear or reset your logs using the "Reset Local Data" feature in Settings, or by uninstalling the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Ihsan does not collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may reach out via our GitHub repository.
